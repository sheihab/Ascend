# Ascend

Your goals, daily plan, focus timer, calendar, progress and reminders in one installable app.

**Live app:** https://sheihab.github.io/ascend/

- **Today**: live focus ring and timer, schedule, daily minimum, goals in focus
- **Goals**: every goal on a timeline with milestones; add, edit, finish, pause or archive
- **Calendar**: past days filled by completion, future plans, goal deadlines, daily notes
- **Progress**: hours by area, weekly trend, 12-month heatmap, levels and badges
- **Plan**: weekly plan versions, periods, books, areas, reminders, calendar export, backups

A block only counts as complete when its focus timer reaches 00:00.

Install: open the live app in Chrome and choose **Install** (Mac) or **Install app** (Android).
Your data stays on your device. Use Plan → Export backup to move it between devices.
